SubCan Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Authentication & Identity Data

When you sign in using Google Identity Services, SubCan receives and verifies your Google subject identifier (sub), your primary email address, and your display name. We use this information strictly to identify your account session and isolate your subscription records.

2. Gmail Mailbox Authorization (Optional)

Connecting your Gmail mailbox requires separate, explicit authorization for https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. SubCan only scans message headers and billing receipts for recurring payment patterns and subscription confirmations. We never sell your personal data or read unassociated personal messages.

3. Data Retention & Isolation

Each user's data is stored with multi-tenant account isolation. You can export or request complete deletion of your records and revoke email authorization at any time directly through the dashboard.
