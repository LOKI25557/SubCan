Overview & Service Architecture

Welcome to SubCan ("we", "us", "our", or "the Service"), accessible at https://subcan-frontend.onrender.com (frontend) with backend API services hosted at https://subcan.onrender.com.

SubCan is an AI-powered subscription cancellation and financial workflow management platform designed to help users detect recurring billing commitments, review unused subscriptions, evaluate inactivity thresholds, and coordinate safe, human-approved cancellations with independent verification of provider renewal states.

Core Safety Philosophy: Detect • Decide • Approve • Cancel • Verify

SubCan operates on an explicit zero-unauthorized-action invariant. SubCan will never automatically cancel a subscription without prior explicit, per-subscription user approval. Furthermore, an automated action’s runtime success is never assumed to be proof of cancellation; independent verification against observed renewal records is mandatory before marking any subscription as cancelled.

How We Process Billing Emails & Detect Subscriptions

When you initiate a mailbox synchronization, SubCan executes an automated, targeted scan designed around privacy and data minimization:

1

Targeted Query Filtering

SubCan does not read your entire inbox. It constructs a narrow, targeted Gmail API search query filtering specifically for billing receipts, invoices, renewal notices, and trusted payment provider domains (e.g., Google Play, Apple App Store, Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay). Spam and trash folders are explicitly excluded.

2

In-Memory Sanitization & Parsing

Retrieved message payloads are decoded strictly in-memory. All executable HTML, scripts, tracking pixels, and external iframe markup are stripped. The parser extracts structured metadata: merchant name, plan description, amount, currency, billing cycle, renewal date, and cancellation signals.

3

Chronological Deduplication & Candidate Generation

Multiple receipts for the same merchant are consolidated chronologically to avoid duplicate records. Extracted data is staged as a Subscription Candidate for your personal review in the dashboard.

4

Local Processing Guarantee (No External AI APIs)

All parsing, regex extraction, inactivity rule evaluation, and decision-making logic is executed locally on SubCan’s backend service. Your email bodies, text snippets, or personal identifiers are never sent to external AI providers (such as OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or Google Gemini APIs).

Data Storage, Retention & Minimization

To prevent privacy sprawl, SubCan enforces strict distinctions between persistent metadata and discarded transient data:

PERSISTENT STORAGE

What Is Stored in Database

  • User Identity: User ID, Google Subject ID, verified email, name, avatar URL.
  • Candidate Records: Merchant name, estimated cost, currency, billing interval, detected renewal date, confidence score, source message ID, email subject, sender, and a short sanitized text snippet (email_snippet).
  • OAuth Tokens: Encrypted Google OAuth access and refresh tokens (in email_accounts) to permit requested mailbox syncs until disconnected.
  • Inventory & Approvals: Confirmed subscriptions, user-edited parameters, and immutable audit events for cancellation runs.
NEVER STORED

What Is Discarded Immediately

  • Full Raw Email Bodies: Complete MIME text/HTML email bodies are processed in-memory and discarded. They are never written to disk or permanent databases.
  • Email Attachments: Invoices attached as PDF, images, or files are not stored on our servers.
  • Non-Billing Emails: Personal messages, newsletters, notifications, and irrelevant correspondence are ignored and discarded.
  • Account Credentials: Google passwords or payment card details are never requested, handled, or stored.

Retention Periods

Stored subscription records and candidate metadata remain accessible for as long as your account remains active. If you disconnect a mailbox, its OAuth credentials are removed immediately. If you request account data deletion, all user-scoped records are permanently purged from the database.

Security Architecture & Multi-Tenant Isolation

We implement comprehensive technical, operational, and organizational security controls to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

OAuth 2.0 with PKCE

Enforces Proof Key for Code Exchange (RFC 7636) and cryptographically random CSRF state tokens to prevent authorization code interception and cross-site injection attacks.

Multi-Tenant Row-Level Isolation

All database operations strictly enforce user boundaries (WHERE user_id = ?). Session tokens cannot inspect, modify, or leak across tenant data boundaries.

Hashed Session Tokens

Server-side sessions are indexed via SHA-256 cryptographic hashes. Raw session tokens are never stored in plain text and are delivered using HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=None cookies.

Human Approval Gate & Idempotency

Every proposed action requires manual user authorization and is protected by cryptographic idempotency keys, preventing accidental re-execution or process duplicate runs.

User Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Frameworks)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area under GDPR and California under CCPA/CPRA), you have specific legal rights regarding your personal information:

Right to Access & Portability: You have the right to request a complete machine-readable copy of your data. SubCan provides an automated JSON data export at GET /api/user/export or directly via the "Export Data" button in your dashboard.
Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and associated records. You can execute this via DELETE /api/user/data or by contacting our privacy contact.
Right to Rectification: You can edit service names, amounts, billing frequencies, renewal dates, and notes for any tracked subscription at any time using the dashboard edit dialog.
Right to Withdraw Consent: You may revoke Gmail mailbox authorization at any moment without penalty. Disconnecting immediately halts email scanning and revokes stored tokens.
California CCPA/CPRA Disclosure: We do not sell personal information or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not discriminate against users who exercise their privacy rights.

Mailbox Disconnection & Account Deletion Procedures

You maintain full, instantaneous control over your connected accounts and stored data through the following mechanisms:

Option A: Disconnect Gmail Mailbox (Self-Service)

To disconnect your email account and stop all mailbox scanning:

  1. Open your SubCan dashboard at /index.html.
  2. Navigate to the Connected Accounts / Discovery panel.
  3. Click Disconnect Mailbox.
Technical Effect: SubCan immediately calls /api/email/disconnect, deletes stored OAuth access and refresh tokens from the database, and executes a best-effort revocation request against Google's token revocation endpoint (https://oauth2.googleapis.com/revoke).

Option B: Revoke via Google Account Permissions

You can independently revoke SubCan's access directly from Google at any time:

  1. Visit your Google Account Security Dashboard: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Select SubCan from the list of third-party apps with account access.
  3. Click Remove Access.
Technical Effect: Google instantly invalidates all refresh tokens issued to SubCan, permanently blocking any further API calls.

Option C: Complete Account & Data Deletion

To permanently purge all records associated with your account:

  1. Click the Export Data button in the dashboard if you wish to retain a personal JSON archive.
  2. Issue a deletion request via the authenticated API: DELETE /api/user/data, or email our privacy contact.
Technical Effect: Deletes all tracked subscriptions, candidate records, action records, audit events, orchestration runs, and email account credentials from the SQLite store.
